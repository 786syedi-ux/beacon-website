Overview

Human Capital Analytics is the market-facing half of workforce decision-making: how does what we pay, how we hire, and how engaged our people are compare to the market — and what does that tell us about what to do next.

We benchmark hiring and retention trends, run and interpret engagement surveys, and assess total reward against the market, so that leadership decisions on headcount, pay and retention are grounded in current data rather than last year's assumptions.
