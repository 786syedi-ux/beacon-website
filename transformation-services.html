Overview

Most HR technology failures aren't about the software — they're about buying a platform before redesigning the workflow it needs to run, or rolling it out without the change management to get it adopted.

We run structured, needs-based platform evaluations, redesign the workflows before automating them, and support implementation through to real adoption — not just go-live.
