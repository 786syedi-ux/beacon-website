Practice · Talent Strategy

Build the leadership pipeline your growth plan depends on.

From first assessment to the C-suite, Talent Strategy is how we make sure the organisation has the right people ready for the next role — not scrambling to find them after someone leaves.

Assessment Development Succession Leadership

Overview

Talent Strategy used to sit as two lines inside a broader HR analytics slide. We've made it a practice in its own right, because the decisions it covers — who gets hired, who gets developed, who's ready to step up — are too consequential to be an afterthought.

We start by diagnosing where the pipeline is thin: which roles have no ready successor, which managers were promoted without being developed for the job, and where assessment data (if it exists at all) isn't being used. From there we build the specific tools — assessment, development pathways, succession plans — that close the gap.
