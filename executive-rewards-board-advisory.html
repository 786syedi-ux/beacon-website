Executive pay is rising again — and boards need to be able to explain why.

Investor and proxy-advisor scrutiny of executive pay has never been higher. We help boards and remuneration committees design pay that's competitive, defensible, and tied to performance.

Overview

Executive Rewards & Board Advisory covers the pay decisions that get read out loud in the annual report — CEO and senior leadership compensation, equity plans, and the governance structures that sit around them.

We benchmark packages against market, help design incentive structures that hold up under say-on-pay scrutiny, and support boards on the governance and ESG questions increasingly attached to how leadership is paid.
