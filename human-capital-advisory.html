Practice · Human Capital Advisory

Most M&A deals fail on people, not paper.

Human Capital Advisory is where we work alongside leadership on the decisions that shape how the organisation is structured, led and run — org design, HR strategy, and the people side of M&A.

Org design M&A readiness Workforce planning Culture

Overview

These are the decisions that don't have a template — how the organisation should be structured for what it's trying to do next, whether the target company's people risk is priced into the deal, what the culture actually needs to become.

We work senior-to-senior with leadership on these questions, because they're too consequential and too specific to hand to a junior team with a generic framework.

What's included

  • 01
    HR Strategy & AlignmentHR strategy and operating model tied directly to business goals, not run in parallel to them.
  • 02
    Org Design & Job EvaluationStructure, role evaluation and job descriptions that support fair grading and pay.
  • 03
    HR MetricsDashboards leadership can actually act on — not vanity metrics.
  • 04
    Vision, Mission, Values & EVPPurpose and employee value proposition, embedded into how people are hired, managed and rewarded.
  • 05

  • 06
    Workforce PlanningForecasting headcount and skill needs ahead of demand, not behind it.
  • 07
    Culture BuildingDiagnosis and practical interventions — leadership behaviour, communication, recognition.

M&A workforce communication, 2025
