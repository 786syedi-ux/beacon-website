Practice · HR Process Audit

Only 13% of organisations say their compliance systems are fully up to date.

An HR Process Audit tells you what's actually happening on the ground — not what the policy manual says should be happening. Most compliance gaps are found here, before a regulator or a lawsuit finds them first.

Risk Compliance Process gaps Strategy fit

Overview

We review HR processes end-to-end — hiring, performance, pay decisions, exits — to find where practice has quietly drifted from policy, and where that drift creates legal, financial or reputational exposure.

The output is a prioritised list of what to fix first, not a 200-page report that sits in a drawer.

What's included

  • 01
    Minimizing RiskCompliance gaps identified and prioritised by actual exposure, not just technical severity.
  • 02
    Aligning ProcessRemoving duplication and closing gaps between what policy says and what managers actually do.
  • 03
    Linking StrategyMaking sure day-to-day HR processes actually deliver on the organisation's stated strategy.
